Quick answer

For most households, the Frigidaire EFIC189 is the best all-around countertop ice maker, blending speed, reliability, and a reasonable footprint.

If you crave soft chewable nuggets, the GE Profile Opal is the top choice; the Igloo ICEB26HNSS wins for bargain hunters; the NewAir AI-100SS is a quiet runner-up; and the Euhomy is a solid editor’s pick for small kitchens.

Countertop ice makers have turned from novelty to necessity for anyone who entertains often or simply hates running out of ice on a hot day. Unlike a freezer’s slow, often-stale cubes, these machines crank out fresh ice in as little as ten minutes and let you choose between bullet, cube, or nugget shapes. The category has exploded in the last few years, so picking the right one means thinking about batch size, noise, and whether you want self-cleaning cycles or Wi‑Fi connectivity. Start by deciding what kind of ice you prefer, bullet ice is fast and cheap, nugget ice is soft and crunchy, and traditional cubes melt slower. Next, consider the ice production rate and storage capacity: most models make between 25 and 50 pounds per day and hold a couple of pounds in the basket. Also weigh whether you’ll use the included scoop and whether the machine fits under your cabinets. A good countertop ice maker should feel like an upgrade, not a compromise.
